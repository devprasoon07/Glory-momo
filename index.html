How the bowl gets built

Twelve minutes,
start to steel bowl

Scroll and it fills. This is the actual order of work behind the counter — the reason regulars budget the wait instead of complaining about it.

01 / 04

  1. Fold

    Nothing is folded before you ask for it. Skin rolled thin, filling judged by eye, pleats closed with one thumb. Twelve momos a minute, and not one of them made this morning.

  2. Steam

    Eight minutes in the aluminium steamer, trays stacked three high. You will smell it from the far end of the food court before we call your number.

  3. Pour the jhol

    Roasted tomato, dry red chilli, garlic, ginger, a spoon of mustard oil, thinned until you can drink it. It goes in straight off the flame, so the bowl arrives too hot to hold.

  4. Finish

    Coriander, a slit green chilli, and the dark chutney on the rim. Served in steel, because paper goes soft before you are halfway through.
